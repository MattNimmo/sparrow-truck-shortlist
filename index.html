How the 100-point ranking works.

Rubric v3.2 ranks the latest fresh saved listings across all five sources. Overall #1 is the highest score. The list recalculates when saved evidence changes; equal scores use deal discount, then stable listing order. Same-seller VIN mirrors count once; separate offers stay separate.

  1. Mover fit · 30 points. Moving body, box length, liftgate, ramp, E-track, side door, suspension and drivetrain preference. A 26-foot moving body earns more than a 24- or 28-foot dry box.
  2. Remaining life · 25 points. Mileage earns up to 20 using a 300,000-mile planning life; model year earns up to 5. Known mileage at or above 300,000 caps the overall score at 59.
  3. Deal · 20 points. Asking price versus a fitted same-channel comparison. Penske and AIM use separate models; unsupported or sparse channels receive neutral 10 points. This is a listing-price model, not an appraisal.
  4. Budget · 15 points. Asking price plus estimated transport to Minneapolis. Full points at $40,000 or less, tapering to zero at $65,000. Unknown totals receive neutral points. Your shopping targets remain $29k–$55k and 80k–170k miles.
  5. Ease · 10 points. Approximate distance from Minneapolis, source access, and a deduction for positive as-is/cosmetic risk language.

Unknowns earn estimated typical points, never a confirmed pass. Each truck shows its assumed-point count and what to verify; open “Point breakdown” for every component. Missing VIN/seller identity is labeled unverified. Price and mileage stay unknown when units are missing. No currency conversions.

Rubric tiers: 90+ STRIKE BUY · 80+ STRONG BUY · 70+ GOOD BUY · 60+ FAIR/WATCH · under 60 PASS. These are scoring labels for investigation priority, not purchase approval. Known incompatible bodies, manual transmissions, overweight/CDL claims, yellow, MaxxForce, sold listings and explicit major condition failures are excluded. Only evidence saved within 24 hours is ranked; incomplete source coverage remains a limitation.
