## Design Context

### Users
Moving-business partners comparing the public top 50 truck listings on desktop and phones. They need to quickly weigh asking price, mileage, moving equipment and the existing weighted rubric before opening seller listings.

### Brand Personality
Practical, warm, decisive. The creator explicitly wants prices larger and among the first things seen, plus an engaging, helpful and colorful comparison.

### Aesthetic Direction
A bright working comparison sheet with Sparrow forest green, warm amber for price/verification attention, and restrained blue for score information. Preserve the established Literata / Public Sans typography. Avoid a long marketing hero or a wall of implementation detail before inventory.

### Design Principles
1. Price and truck identity are visible without horizontal scrolling.
2. Show all five actual metric scores; color always has a text label.
3. Put detailed source claims and scoring evidence behind an accessible expansion control.
4. Keep rank stable while sorting/filtering; preserve unknowns and identity warnings.
5. Maintain live updates and public data limits; change presentation, not ranking weights.
